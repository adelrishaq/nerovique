The files are available immediately after payment. Privacy and cookie choices

This site serves its pages, sample images and styles from its own host. It has no account form or mailing-list form, and payment happens on Polar Checkout, not on this site. Opening the guide and using an extracted worksheet does not send worksheet answers to Nerovique. If you send a support email, your mail provider transmits that message. Include the file name and device and omit payment credentials and customer records.

The Cookie settings control stores your selection in this browser. You may decline marketing and use the site, or reopen the control to withdraw permission. Clearing site storage resets the choice. On HTTPS pages, choosing Allow loads the Meta Pixel for Nerovique and requests a PageView event. Meta may receive the page URL, browser and network information and set advertising cookies. Withdrawing permission stops future pixel loading on this site. Data already transmitted is outside this browser control.

When you buy, Polar processes the payment and collects the details needed for the order, such as your name, email address, billing country and payment information, under its own privacy policy. Nerovique receives the order details it needs to deliver the files, answer support requests and send the order confirmation email: your name, email address, country, order reference and the consent choice you made at checkout. Card details are handled by Polar and its payment processor and never reach Nerovique.

Support emails and order copies are kept for up to 90 days after a case is closed, attachments for up to 30 days, and temporary exports for up to 7 days. Polar keeps its own order and tax records under its privacy policy. To ask for a copy or deletion of your data, email nerovique.support@gmail.com.

Using an external AI assistant is optional. Any text you submit to that service is processed under its own terms. Practice with fictional inputs and remove identifiers, order data and credentials before sharing prompts.

Refund requests

Nerovique offers no voluntary refund after successful digital delivery. If the files were not delivered, are defective, or you were charged twice, contact nerovique.support@gmail.com with the order reference so the issue can be investigated and the appropriate remedy provided. Mandatory consumer rights and Polar's applicable checkout and refund terms continue to apply.

Include the file name and a short description. Keep card details out of support emails.

Support and updates

Use nerovique.support@gmail.com for file access, a damaged download or a reproducible workbook or worksheet problem. Include the edition, file, device or browser, expected result and what you observed. A fictional example helps us reproduce a calculation issue.

Support covers the supplied files. Store implementation, custom consulting and third-party AI platform support are not included. Support is provided by email in English.
