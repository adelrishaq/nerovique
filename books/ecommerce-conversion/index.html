Inside chapter six

What will this basket actually cost?

A fictional coffee offer leaves $24 per order today and $21 with a new shipping offer. How many orders would cover the difference?

100 × $24 = $2,400

At $21 each, 114 orders fall $6 short. The 115th clears the target. Use the worksheet to change the carrier cost and check the threshold yourself.

Using the guide

Before you begin

Can I use the worksheets offline?

Yes. Extract the ZIP, open START-HERE.html, then choose a worksheet. Save or print your answers before closing the file.

Do I need Shopify or an AI subscription?

You can complete the fictional cases on paper or in the local forms. An assistant is optional. Shopify Sidekick has its own instructions, and availability depends on the platform.

How do I use the AI exercises?

Choose one chapter, read its evidence and copy the instructions from the named skill. Compare the draft answer with the supplied records before recording a decision.

Is a sales increase guaranteed?

No. The guide does not guarantee sales, conversions, revenue or profit. It shows how to find where a purchase breaks down, plan one change, test it and decide whether the result justifies keeping it.
